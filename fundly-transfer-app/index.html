Fundly: move money between your accounts

A prototype of a mobile banking transfer flow, with a fictional bank and invented rules. Try a transfer, then switch the time of day to see how the 8:00 PM cutoff changes what the customer is told.

Demo time (Thursday, Oct 15)

Invented rules

  • Transfers between your own accounts made before 8:00 PM ET arrive instantly.
  • After 8:00 PM ET they arrive the next business day by 6:00 AM ET.
  • Limit: $5,000 per day.
  • Money you move out is held right away, even when it arrives later.
