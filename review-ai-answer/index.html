Decision scenario for support agents

Review the AI's answer

At Northlight, an AI assistant named Adelaide drafts replies to customers. You decide whether each draft goes out as written, gets edited, or goes to someone else. Five scenes, about five minutes.

Learning objectives

By the end you can:

  • Compare an AI draft with the source before it reaches a customer
  • Match how confident a reply sounds to what the evidence supports
  • Choose between sending, editing, and escalating, and tell customers the truth either way
Designed by Daniel Cooperider. Northlight, Adelaide, and every policy shown here are fictional and were invented for this demonstration. They are not real policies or advice.
