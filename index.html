Background

Why psychology

I studied psychology because I wanted to understand people and their motivations. I studied marketing next, to understand what they respond to. Both show up in how I build training.

  • I start with the person. What are they trying to get done, and what gets in the way?
  • I look at behavior after training, using surveys, check-ins, and KPI data, because attendance only shows who came.
  • I make practice safe. A simulation lets someone try a response, get it wrong, and see what happens before it costs anything.
